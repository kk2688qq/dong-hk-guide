# HK-014 条件录取 vs 无条件录取：先核对清楚再付款

<!-- 风险=高 阶段=怎么申请 焦虑=确定性 -->

**说人话**

Offer 分两种，有条件录取和无条件录取。有条件的意思是孩子还没达标，要交证明文件证明达标，学校才确认。留位费和接受费普遍不可退，唯一常见的例外是「未达 offer 条件」，科大和城大官方明文可退，港大 FAQ 也这么说，中大官方口径偏「一律不可退」。所以付款前第一件事，是看清 offer 上写了什么条件。

**要花什么**

- **钱**：留位费 / 接受费**按校按课差很多**（无统一金额），且**多数情况不退**。
- **时间**：接受 offer 后通常 **2–3 周内**要付款（中大英文系官方口径）；港大国际本科项目是「**三至四周内**」表态。
- **精力**：**逐字读 offer letter 的条件**。
- **要核对的硬项**：① offer 是**有条件还是无条件**；② **条件具体是什么**（毕业证 / 成绩 / 语言分）；③ **付款截止日**；④ **退款条款**（尤其「未达条件」是否可退）。

**换回什么**

换回的是「**不白丢一笔留位费**」——清楚「什么情况能退、什么情况不能退」，也避免把「有条件」误当「已录取」而**放下其他申请**。

**规则一：有条件录取要先「达成条件」**

科大 MSc(MAFM) 官方原文：

> 「In order to confirm the offer for registration, **applicants with conditional offers must submit documentary proof of having met the conditions** as early as possible and no later than **mid-July for Fall admission, or the end of December for Spring admission.** They will see **'Conditions met'** in their program choice status in the Online Admissions System once fulfillment is confirmed.」

港大国际招生页原文：

> 「Our advice is to apply for a visa as soon as you have received a **firm or conditional offer.**」

**规则二：留位费 / 接受费多数不可退**

**港大 · TPG FAQ**

> 「**Is the deposit payment refundable and transferable?**　**Deposit is non-refundable unless you do not meet the conditions specified in the offer of acceptance.**」
> 「If I cancel/withdraw my application, can I get a refund of my application fee? —— **The application fee is non-refundable and non-transferable once submitted.**」

**中大 · How to Apply（Admission Offer Process）**

> 「2. **Accept offer and pay admission deposit** according to the instruction specified in the Payment Advice. 3. If you receive several offers, **you can only accept one. Decline previous offers before accepting a new one.** 4. **All fees paid are non-refundable and non-transferable.**」

**中大 · 研究生总则 3.1**

> 「An applicant who has been offered admission to a course of study shall **pay the fees and register by the respective deadlines. Anyone who fails to pay fees and register by the specified deadline shall be considered to have declined the offer.**」

**中大 · 英文系 Admission FAQ**

> 「Successful applicants will have to pay the deposit **two to three weeks after the admission offer is sent to you**…… The **offer acceptance and payment due date CANNOT be delayed under any circumstances.**」
> 「Can I apply for a refund of deposit? —— **According to the University's policy, the deposit paid CANNOT be refunded.**」

**理大 · MSc(VSI) 学位页**

> 「Upon receiving an admission offer, applicants are required to pay the **non-refundable initial registration fee to confirm their acceptance.**」
> 「Failure to do so or any discrepancies found may lead to **nullification of their admission offers and registrations at PolyU.**」

**城大 · Apply Now（三种例外）**

> 「**Acceptance Fee（non-refundable and non-transferable）**　To accept the offer, applicants must confirm their acceptance online and pay the acceptance fee in full by the reply deadline. **Failure to do so will be regarded as declining the offer.** Acceptance fees are non-refundable and non-transferable, **except under the following circumstances:**
> • when the applicant subsequently accepts another offer of an award programme of CityUHK in the same semester/term of admission and has paid the relevant acceptance fee;
> • **when the applicant fails to meet the condition(s) of the admission offer resulting in a withdrawal of the offer by CityUHK;**
> • when the programme for which an offer is made is subsequently cancelled by CityUHK.」

**科大 · MSc(MAFM) Accepting Offer**

> 「**In case I fail to meet the conditions, will the University refund the deposit to me?**　**The deposit paid will be refunded if an applicant provides proof that he/she has failed to meet the conditions of the offer. Otherwise, any deposit paid is non-refundable.**」

**⚠️ 关键差异：不是每所学校都「未达条件就能退」**

- **科大**：官方明文——**未达条件可退**（须提供证明）。
- **城大**：官方明文列三种例外，其中包含**「未达 offer 条件、导致城大撤回 offer」**。
- **港大 · TPG FAQ**：明文——**「Deposit is non-refundable unless you do not meet the conditions specified in the offer」**。
- **中大**：官方口径偏「**All fees paid are non-refundable and non-transferable**」「**the deposit paid CANNOT be refunded**」——**未见「未达条件可退」的明文**。
- **结论**：**必须分校分写，不能一概说「都能退」。**

**⚠️ 官方没有「定义条款」**

- **「Conditional Offer / Firm Offer」的正式定义**：五校官方页**未找到统一定义条款**。官方多以**流程描述**呈现（科大「Conditions met」、港大「firm or conditional offer」）。
- **条件内容以 offer letter 为准**。正文不给「定义」，只讲**怎么核对**。
- **留位费金额**：**无统一**——按校按课不同（科大 MPP 押金 **HK$80,000**；港大 MSc(CS) 非本地押金 **HK$167,400** 等），**须逐课引用，不得给统一金额**。

**红线提醒**

- **付款前把 offer letter 的条件逐条读一遍**——有条件录取**没达成条件就不算确定**。
- **别只看「录取」两个字**——要看是**conditional**还是**firm**。
- **别在同一校接受两个 offer**——中大官方明文「**you can only accept one. Decline previous offers before accepting a new one.**」
- **别错过付款截止**——逾期即视为**放弃 offer**（中大总则 3.1：「**shall be considered to have declined the offer**」；城大：「**Failure to do so will be regarded as declining the offer.**」）。
- **别以为所有学校「未达条件都能退」**——**中大官方口径偏不可退**（见上）。

**常见误传**

1. **「拿到录取就稳了，可以拒其他学校」**——**不成立**。若是**有条件录取**，须**提交证明达成条件**（科大原文），未达成则 offer 可能被撤回。
2. **「留位费在任何情况都能退」**——**错**。五校普遍写明**不可退 / 不可转让**；只有**特定例外**（科大、城大明文含「未达条件」）。
3. **「所有学校未达条件都能退留位费」**——**不准确**。**科大、城大、港大**有明文；**中大官方口径偏「一律不退」**，**未见例外条款**。
4. **「可以先交钱，之后再说」**——**代价很高**。留位费**金额可能很大**（如科大 MPP 押金 **HK$80,000**），且**多数不退**。
5. **「接受 offer 后还能慢慢补材料」**——**有硬期限**。中大官方：「offer acceptance and payment due date **CANNOT be delayed under any circumstances.**」

**适用人群 + 入学年度**

- **谁该看**：已收到（或即将收到）港校 offer、准备付款的学生与家长。
- **入学年度**：不适用（本条属录取与付款流程）。

**解决什么焦虑 + 风险等级 + 决策阶段**

- **风险等级**：高——留位费金额可能达**数万至十余万**且多数不退，误判「有条件 / 无条件」会直接造成**重大金钱损失**，还可能**错过其他机会**。
- **解决什么焦虑**：确定性（这份 offer 到底算什么、钱能不能退）。
- **决策阶段**：怎么申请。

**关键节点与时效**

- **达成条件的期限**：科大 MSc(MAFM)——秋季入学 **7 月中旬**、春季入学 **12 月底**前提交证明。
- **付款期限**：中大英文系——收到 offer 后 **2–3 周**内；港大国际本科项目——**3–4 周**内表态。
- **接受费 / 留位费**：**按校按课不同**，且**多数不可退**。

**证据等级 + 官方依据**

证据等级：**A**（各校官方页原文）；**「术语无统一定义」＝官方无明文的结论，已显式注明**

**官方尚未公布／未写死的事项**

- **留位费统一金额**：**无统一**——按校按课不同，须逐课引用。
- **「未达条件一定退留位费」**：**非普适**——科大 / 城大 / 港大有明文，**中大偏不可退**；正文已分校分写。
